About MissingPawn

What is MissingPawn?

MissingPawn is an open-source chess engine written in C++ and designed for use with UCI-compatible chess software. It is an actively developed experimental project focused on search, evaluation, tactical and positional play, and the study of pawn structure.

The project explores how pawn structure and limitations of opponent options can influence position evaluation and engine behavior. This direction is presented as an experimental research path for future development.

Project Focus

MissingPawn is intended as a practical and extensible chess engine platform for study, engine testing, and development.

  • Search and move generation
  • Evaluation and position scoring
  • Pawn structure analysis
  • Tactical and positional experimentation
